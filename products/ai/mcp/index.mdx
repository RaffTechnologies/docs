<sub>Updated October 11, 2026</sub>

The Raff MCP server lets an AI assistant work in your Raff account. Connect `https://mcp.rafftechnologies.com/mcp` to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude Code or any app that supports the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io), sign in with your Raff account once, and ask for what you need: "give me a Postgres database for this app", "why is this query slow", "add an index on orders.created_at". Managed databases come first; apps, functions and servers follow.

## What you can ask

| You ask | The assistant does |
|---|---|
| "Create a free Postgres database for my side project" | Creates it, waits until it accepts connections and gives you the connection string |
| "Show me the tables in my shop database" | Lists the tables and describes their columns |
| "Which queries are slowest, and what index would help?" | Reads the slow query list and suggests an index; adds it if you agree |
| "Upgrade the database to 2 vCPU" | Shows the new monthly price and the card it is paid with, and changes nothing until you confirm |
| "Back up the database, then restore last night's backup into a copy" | Takes a backup and restores into a new database, leaving the original running |
| "How do I connect to Raff Postgres from Prisma?" | Answers from the Raff documentation |

Paid changes always show the price first and wait for your confirmation. See [Paid changes](/products/ai/mcp/concepts/paid-changes).
