<sub>Updated September 7, 2026</sub>

The single-master control plane is free. You pay per worker node, plus \$30/month for the optional HA control plane. Persistent storage is not part of the cluster price — PersistentVolumeClaims are billed per GB as you create them. Prices below are per node; live values come from the [pricing API](/api-reference/kubernetes/list-node-plans).

## Worker node plans

Two CPU tiers, same convention as [VM plans](/products/build/virtual-machines/details/pricing): **Shared** for general workloads, **CPU-Optimized** with a much higher CPU commitment for steady, compute-heavy workloads (databases, CI runners, batch processing).

Every worker has a 25 GB NVMe root disk for the OS, container images, logs and ephemeral storage. Persistent volumes are [Raff Volumes provisioned by your PVCs](/products/build/kubernetes/concepts/storage), priced below.

## Add-ons

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| HA control plane (3 masters, 2 gateways) | \$30.00/month |
| Persistent volume storage (`raff-block` PVCs) | \$0.10/GB/month, billed hourly (10 GB–1 TB per volume) |

You pay for exactly the storage your claims request — every GB billed is a GB you can use. Pay-as-you-go bills hourly per existing node; subscriptions commit the node set monthly, with PVC storage always billed hourly on top — see [Billing model](/products/build/kubernetes/concepts/billing-model). Kubernetes itself uses about 1 GB of memory per node — prefer fewer, larger nodes over many small ones.
