<sub>Updated October 15, 2026</sub>

Prices are in USD per server, region US East. Every price includes the Windows Server licence (\$15.00 a month). Installing the stack is included; there is no setup fee. See [Billing model](/products/build/raff-keep/concepts/billing-model).

## Raff Guardian

The **Summary** shows Raff Guardian as "Raff Guardian: free for the first 6 months, then \$10.00 a month" for every choice except **Install only**. It is not part of what you pay at checkout. See [Kept by Raff](/products/build/raff-keep/concepts/raff-guardian-choice).

## Included

- The install, its checks and the receipt of every step.
- Adding terminals, sites, domains and .NET versions later.
- SQL Server Express on an ASP.NET server (free from Microsoft, up to 10 GB per database).
- Let's Encrypt certificates for your domains.
