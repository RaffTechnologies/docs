<sub>Updated October 19, 2026</sub>

Prices are in USD per instance, region US East. An app with three instances costs three times its size's price. HTTPS, custom domains and pull request previews are included. See [Billing model](/products/build/raff-apps/concepts/billing-model).

## Sizes

| Size | vCPU | Memory | Temporary disk | Per hour | Per month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Micro | 0.25 | 512 MB | 2 GB | \$0.00411 | \$3.00 |
| Starter | 0.5 | 1 GB | 5 GB | \$0.009575 | \$6.99 |
| Standard | 1 | 2 GB | 10 GB | \$0.023274 | \$16.99 |
| Pro | 2 | 4 GB | 20 GB | \$0.038342 | \$27.99 |
| Pro Max | 4 | 8 GB | 40 GB | \$0.084918 | \$61.99 |
| Scale | 8 | 16 GB | 80 GB | \$0.16437 | \$119.99 |

The monthly price is the hourly price for 730 hours.

## Monthly or pay-as-you-go

| | Monthly (the default) | Pay-as-you-go |
|---|---|---|
| How you pay | The monthly price per instance, at checkout | Per second of running time, from your account balance |
| Instances above the plan | Billed per second at the hourly price | Every instance is billed per second |
| A sleeping app (scale to zero) | Plan paid in advance | Nothing while it sleeps |
| Delete | The unused part of the month goes to your balance | Billing stops |

On pay-as-you-go, a cron or job app costs only while a run is going, and a paused app costs nothing.

## Postgres for an app

Add a managed PostgreSQL database when you create the app, or link one later. It is billed as a database, on its own.

| Plan | vCPU | Memory | Storage | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Postgres, free | 1 | 1 GB | 2 GB | Free. One per account, pauses after 7 days without connections. |
| Postgres | 1 | 1 GB | 25 GB | \$7.99 per month |

Bigger plans and high availability: see [Managed Databases pricing](/products/store/databases/details/pricing).

## Spend cap

| Setting | Default | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly cap | On, \$50 | \$1 to \$1,000,000, or off |
| At the cap | Keep apps running and email me | Or pause pay-as-you-go apps |
| Emails | 75%, 90% and 100% of the cap | To account owners and billing contacts |

The cap counts pay-as-you-go instances and the instances of monthly apps above their plan. Monthly plans paid at checkout and databases are not counted.
