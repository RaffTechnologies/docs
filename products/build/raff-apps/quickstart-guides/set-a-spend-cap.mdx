<sub>Updated October 19, 2026</sub>

The spend cap is a monthly limit on what your apps cost. It is on for every account from the start, at $50 a month, and only sends emails. You can change the amount, turn it off, or have it pause your apps when it is reached.

## How the cap works

- It covers all the apps in your account, for each calendar month.
- It counts usage: pay-as-you-go apps, and apps on a monthly plan only for what they run above their plan. The price of a monthly plan paid at checkout does not count.
- The account owners and billing contacts get an email and a notice in the dashboard at 75% and 90% of the cap, and again when it is reached.
- Raff checks spending every few minutes, so usage can pass the cap by a little before apps pause.

## Set the cap

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Apps page">
    Go to **Apps**. The bar at the top shows what you spent this month and the cap, for example "$12.40 spent this month · cap $50, email only".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the cap settings">
    Click **Manage cap**.

    <Frame>
      ![TODO: Monthly spend cap dialog with the amount and the two choices](/images/products/build/raff-apps/spend-cap-dialog.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the amount">
    Leave **Spend cap enabled** on and enter the **Monthly cap (USD)**, at least $1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what happens at the cap">
    - **Keep apps running and email me**: apps keep serving past the cap. The bar shows **Over cap, still running**.
    - **Pause my apps**: pay-as-you-go apps stop until next month, or until you raise the cap. Apps on a monthly plan keep running unless they run above their plan. Your data is kept.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The new cap applies at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove the cap, turn **Spend cap enabled** off and click **Save**. Raff keeps the amount and choice for when you turn it on again.

## When the cap pauses your apps

The bar on **Apps** shows **Paused by cap**. Raff stops:

- Pay-as-you-go apps, and apps on a monthly plan that run above their plan.
- Cron job and one-off job runs that are in progress.
- [Pull request previews](/products/build/raff-apps/quickstart-guides/preview-pull-requests) of the paused apps.

While they are paused, resuming, creating, deploying, scaling up and running jobs of pay-as-you-go apps is refused. Raise the cap or turn it off, and Raff starts the apps it paused within seconds. Apps you paused yourself stay paused.

## Do it from your tools

`$RAFF_API_KEY` is an [API key](/products/manage/team-projects/quickstart-guides/generate-api-key). The cap is set for the whole account.

**API**: [Get spend cap settings](/api-reference/apps/get-app-spend-settings), [Update spend cap settings](/api-reference/apps/update-app-spend-settings), [Get Apps usage](/api-reference/apps/get-apps-usage)

```bash
# Read the cap; paused_by_cap is true while the cap has apps paused
curl "https://api.rafftechnologies.com/api/v1/apps/spend-settings" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $RAFF_API_KEY"

# Cap at $100 a month and pause apps when it is reached
curl -X PATCH "https://api.rafftechnologies.com/api/v1/apps/spend-settings" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $RAFF_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"enabled": true, "monthly_cap_usd": 100, "cap_action": "pause"}'

# Turn the cap off
curl -X PATCH "https://api.rafftechnologies.com/api/v1/apps/spend-settings" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $RAFF_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"enabled": false}'

# Spending this month, per app
curl "https://api.rafftechnologies.com/api/v1/apps/usage" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $RAFF_API_KEY"
```

Send only the fields you want to change. `cap_action` is `notify_only` or `pause`. While apps are paused by the cap, the refused calls return `412`.

**CLI**

```bash
# Spending this month, per app
raff apps usage
```
