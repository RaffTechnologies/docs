## Uptime commitment

Raff Technologies guarantees **99.90% monthly uptime** for the services below.

| Service | Monthly uptime | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Virtual Machines | 99.90% | |
| Block Storage (Volumes) | 99.90% | |
| Snapshots | 99.90% | |
| Backups | 99.90% | |
| Object Storage | 99.90% | S3 API availability |
| VPC Networking & Floating IPs | 99.90% | |
| Managed Kubernetes — nodes | 99.90% | Worker and storage nodes, covered as Virtual Machines |
| Managed Kubernetes — endpoint & ingress path | 99.90% | Clusters with the **High Availability option** (see below) |
| Managed Databases | 99.90% | Instances with the **High Availability option** (see below) |

Uptime is calculated as `(total minutes in the month - unavailable minutes) / total minutes × 100%`.

### Managed Kubernetes

- **Worker and storage nodes** are Virtual Machines and carry the 99.90% VM commitment.
- **The managed endpoint and ingress path** (public endpoint, load balancing, Kubernetes API reachability) carry the 99.90% commitment on clusters running the **High Availability option**, where the endpoint is a redundant gateway pair with automatic failover and the control plane runs 3 nodes.
- On **non-HA clusters** the endpoint is a single managed instance: it is covered at the infrastructure (VM) level, without a separate endpoint-path commitment.
- Workloads, Ingress resources, and add-on configuration inside the cluster are customer-managed and outside the SLA.

### Managed Databases

- Instances with the **High Availability option** (replicated, automatic failover) carry the 99.90% commitment on the database endpoint.
- **Single-node instances** are covered at the infrastructure (VM) level, without a separate endpoint commitment.

**Unavailability** means the service is completely inaccessible — returns errors or doesn't respond to API or console requests — for **more than 5 consecutive minutes**. The status page is the authoritative source for measurement.

## Service credit schedule

If monthly uptime falls below 99.90%, you become eligible for a service credit on the affected service:

| Monthly uptime | Service credit |
|---|---|
| 99.90% — 99.0% | **10%** of that service's monthly charges |
| 99.0% — 95.0% | **25%** of that service's monthly charges |
| Below 95.0% | **100%** of that service's monthly charges |

Notes on credits:

- Capped at 100% of the affected service's monthly charges for the billing cycle.
- **Not refundable to your card** — credits apply against future charges only.
- **Expire after 12 months** from issuance, or on account termination, whichever comes first.
- Service credits are the **sole and exclusive remedy** for any failure to meet the uptime commitment.

## What's excluded

The 99.90% commitment does not apply when unavailability is caused by:

- **Scheduled maintenance** announced at least **24 hours** in advance by email or platform notification
- **Emergency security patches or critical maintenance** requiring less than 10 minutes of downtime
- Customer error or misuse of the service
- Failures in customer-installed software, OS, or configuration
- **Customer-managed workloads and configuration** inside Managed Kubernetes clusters or Managed Databases (applications, Ingress resources, database queries and schema)
- Third-party certificate authorities (e.g. Let's Encrypt issuance delays)
- **DDoS attacks** against your resources or the platform
- Force majeure (natural disasters, war, terrorism)
- Internet outages or third-party service failures beyond Raff's reasonable control
- Accounts with overdue payments, suspended accounts, or accounts in violation of the Terms of Service

## How to claim a credit

1. **Within 30 days** of the incident, contact [support@rafftechnologies.com](mailto:support@rafftechnologies.com).
2. Include:
   - Date, time, and duration of the unavailability
   - The specific service(s) affected (e.g. Virtual Machines, Managed Kubernetes)
   - Your account ID
3. After validation, the service credit appears on your account in the next billing cycle.

The status page at [rafftechnologies.com/status](https://rafftechnologies.com/status) records every incident automatically — referencing the relevant entry in your request speeds up validation.
