title: Create a database

---

<sub>Updated October 11, 2026</sub>

Create a database by picking an engine, a plan and how it connects. A free or pay-as-you-go database starts within a minute; a monthly database starts right after checkout.

## Before you start

- A Raff account and a project to create the database in (the project picker is at the top left).
- For a paid plan on monthly billing, a card. Free databases need no card.
- Optional: a VPC, if your VMs or apps should reach the database privately. You can create one in the same form.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the create page">
    Go to **Databases** and click **Create database**, or click **+ Create** at the top right and choose **Managed Database**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identity: engine, name, version">
    Pick the engine: **PostgreSQL** (apps, APIs, AI), **MySQL** (WordPress, Laravel), **Valkey** (cache, sessions, queues), **ClickHouse** (analytics, logs) or **Kafka** (event streams).

    Give it a **Name**, for example `orders-db`. Names are unique within the project. Pick the **Version**; the newest is preselected. The region is **US East**.

    <Frame>
      ![TODO: Identity card with engine cards, name and version](/images/products/store/databases/create-01-identity.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compute & storage: pick a plan">
    Pick a row in the plan table: **Compute**, **RAM**, **NVMe storage**, **Connections** and **Price**. The **Free** plan is preselected when your account has no free database yet.

    On a paid plan you can add storage in 10 GB steps with **+** ($0.15 per GB per month). Storage can grow later without downtime, but never shrink. See [Plans and sizing](/products/store/databases/concepts/plans-and-sizing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engine options">
    - **PostgreSQL, Turn on now**: `vector` (AI embeddings) is ticked; tick `pg_trgm` for fuzzy text search. More extensions can be turned on later in **Settings**.
    - **Valkey, Cache or queue?**: **Cache** removes the least recently used keys when memory is full; **Queue or data** (default) never removes keys, and new writes fail when memory is full.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Topology">
    **Single node** for development and staging, or **High availability** for production: copies on separate servers with automatic failover. High availability adds 70% to the plan price (140% for MySQL and Kafka) and is not available on the free plan. For MySQL, choose it now: it cannot be added to a MySQL database later. See [High availability and replicas](/products/store/databases/concepts/high-availability-and-replicas).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Network">
    - **Use existing VPC** or **Create new VPC**: the database gets a private address inside the VPC, reachable from your VMs and apps on it.
    - **Skip for now, no VPC**: reach the database from the dashboard and, after you turn it on, over its public address.

    Public access is turned on after create, in the **Access** tab. See [Turn on public access](/products/store/databases/quickstart-guides/turn-on-public-access).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Check the **Estimated price** on the right, then:

    | Your choice | Button | What happens |
    |---|---|---|
    | Free plan | **Create free database** | Starts at once, no payment |
    | Paid plan, pay-as-you-go account | **Create database** | Starts at once, billed by the hour from your balance |
    | Paid plan, monthly account | **Continue to checkout** | Pay the first month, then it starts |
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify

The **Creating your database** screen shows each step and turns into **Your database is ready** with its connection string. In the **Databases** list, its status is **Healthy**.

A database with public access shows as ready only once its public address accepts connections, so the connection string works the moment you see it.
