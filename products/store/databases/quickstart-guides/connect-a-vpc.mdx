<sub>Updated October 11, 2026</sub>

Connecting a VPC puts a private endpoint for the database inside your VPC, with the stable hostname `<database_id>.db.raffusercloud.com`. Your VMs on that VPC connect to it directly, with no public address and no egress charges.

## Before you start

- A running database.
- A VPC with the VMs that should reach the database. See [VPC](/products/network/vpc).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Access">
    Open the database and go to the **Access** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the VPC">
    In **Private networking**, choose the VPC in **Select a VPC...** and click **Connect VPC**. It can take a few minutes the first time, while the VPC's router starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect from a VM">
    Go to **Connect**, choose **Your VPC**, and copy the address. From a VM on the VPC:

    ```bash
    psql "postgresql://raffadmin:PASSWORD@a1b2c3d4.db.raffusercloud.com:6432/defaultdb?sslmode=require"
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also pick a VPC when you [create the database](/products/store/databases/quickstart-guides/create-a-database).

## Disconnect

In **Private networking**, click **Disconnect** and confirm. The data is untouched and public access stays as it was. Connecting a VPC again gives the endpoint a new private IP; the hostname stays the same.

## Do it from your tools

- **API**: [`POST /api/v1/databases/{database_id}/vpc`](/api-reference/databases/connect-database-v-p-c), [`DELETE`](/api-reference/databases/disconnect-database-v-p-c)
- **CLI**: [`raff database vpc`](/reference/cli/database)
