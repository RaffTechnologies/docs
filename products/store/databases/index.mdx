<sub>Updated October 11, 2026</sub>

Raff Managed Databases run PostgreSQL, MySQL, Valkey, ClickHouse and Kafka for you: Raff sets them up, backs them up every night, applies failover when you turn on high availability, and watches them around the clock. A database is ready in seconds, reachable from your VPC or over the internet with TLS, and every plan includes NVMe storage with no egress fees. One PostgreSQL, MySQL or Valkey database per account is free.
