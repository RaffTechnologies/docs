<sub>Updated October 11, 2026</sub>

Prices are in USD per database per month, region US East. Backups, monitoring, TLS and traffic are included; there are no egress charges. Pay-as-you-go accounts pay the same by the hour, up to the monthly price. See [Billing model](/products/store/databases/concepts/billing-model).

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price | Engines |
|---|---|---|
| High availability | +70% of the plan | PostgreSQL, Valkey, ClickHouse |
| High availability | +140% of the plan (3 nodes) | MySQL, Kafka |
| Read replica | The plan price, per replica | PostgreSQL |
| Extra storage | \$0.15 per GB per month, in 10 GB steps | All paid plans |
